She's Coming Back!

After being inactive for months, Billie Eilish has finally finished the new movie she is starring in, The Bell Jar. What does this mean for fans?

New Concert, Maybe New Music?

Billie Eilish seemingly hasn't been working on anything new after the movie that was released, but apparently, she kept secrets from us all. Only a few days ago did the singer-songwriter, along with her beloved brother, Finneas O'Connell, announce an exclusive concert for Los Angeles fans. People in the area are overjoyed about the unexpected news. There is a mix of surprise, excitement, but even some disappointment from fans in other countries.

Los Angeles is Billie Eilish's hometown, so it makes sense she decided to come up with this idea. Fans have been complaining about the lack of activity on Eilish's social media pages, and how long it usually takes her to release new music. But this was absolutely a wonderful surprise, and will most definitely bring the fan-base back together. However, fans from other continents like Europe and Asia are complaining that she never hosts any events in their countries.

But Eilish has expressed her enthusiasm for this upcoming concert. I'm really excited to be doing this after months of taking a break from music and focusing on something different. I can't wait to see my fans after so long, said the singer. It's clear that Billie Eilish is excited to finally sing again, but can this also mean new music will be uploaded? We don't know yet, but we can't wait to find out!

The concert will be held at Crypto.com Arena on November 11 this year!
